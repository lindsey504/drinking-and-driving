How It Works

🏌️ The Draft

  • Each team drafts 15 players from the player pool.
  • Snake draft order — take turns picking until all rosters are full.
  • Once a player is drafted, no one else can pick them.

📅 Weekly Lineups

  • Before each tournament, set your 5 starters from your 15-player roster.
  • Lineups lock when the tournament starts — no swapping mid-round.
  • If you forget to set a lineup, your previous week's starters carry over.

🏆 Scoring

  • Your team score = combined score-to-par of your 5 starters.
  • Lower is better — this is stroke play.
  • Players who miss the cut count as +10 for that tournament.
  • Majors (Masters, US Open, The Open, PGA Championship) count 1.5×.

📊 Season Standings

  • The team with the best score each tournament wins that week.
  • Season winner = most wins. Points are the tiebreaker.

⛳ Majors (1.5× multiplier)

  • The Masters
  • US Open
  • The Open Championship
  • PGA Championship
